Rooted in Pen

A tradition of artistry and reverence

Bhumi Kala Kendra is a Ganpati idol business based in Pen, Raigad, Maharashtra. The business focuses on Shadu Mati Ganesh murtis, bringing together traditional craftsmanship, devotion and an appreciation for environmentally conscious materials.

Each murti reflects the cultural importance of Ganesh Chaturthi and the artistic heritage associated with Pen.
